Nakakuha ka na ng agency at may employer na interesado sa iyo. Ang next step? Ang **INTERVIEW**.

Para sa maraming OFW applicants, ito ang pinaka-nerve-wracking part. Pero with proper preparation, pwede mong i-ace ang interview at makuha ang dream job mo abroad.

## Bago ang Interview

### 1. Research ang Employer at Country

Alamin ang basics tungkol sa:

- Ano ang work ng employer? (Family? Company? Hospital?)
- Ano ang culture ng bansang pupuntahan mo?
- Ano ang basic laws para sa workers doon?

### 2. Practice Your English (o Arabic Basics)

Karamihan ng interviews ay in English. Practice speaking clearly at confidently.

Para sa Middle East, matuto ng basic Arabic greetings:

- **Marhaba** = Hello
- **Shukran** = Thank you
- **Na'am** = Yes
- **La** = No

### 3. Prepare Your Documents

Kahit video call ang interview, have these ready:

- Resume/CV
- Certificates
- Valid ID
- Photos

## Sa Mismong Interview

### DO's:

- Dress neatly — kahit video call, magpakita ng professionalism
- Smile at make eye contact (sa camera kung video call)
- Speak clearly at moderate pace
- Be honest — kung hindi mo alam, sabihin mo at willing kang matuto
- Ask questions — nagpapakita ito ng genuine interest

### DON'Ts:

- Huwag mag-lie tungkol sa experience o skills
- Huwag maging over-familiar — keep it professional
- Huwag pag-usapan ang salary agad — let them bring it up
- Huwag badmouth ang previous employer

## Common Interview Questions (at Paano Sagutin)

**Q: "Tell me about yourself."**

A: Give a brief intro — name, experience, skills, at bakit ka interested sa job. Keep it under 2 minutes.

**Q: "Why do you want to work abroad?"**

A: Be honest pero professional. Pwede mong sabihin: *"I want to provide a better life for my family while gaining international experience."*

**Q: "Can you work long hours?"**

A: *"Yes, I understand the demands of the job and I am prepared to work the required hours."*

**Q: "Do you have experience with elderly care / children / cooking?"**

A: If yes, give specific examples. If no, say you're willing to learn at may related experience ka.

**Q: "When can you start?"**

A: *"I can start as soon as all my documents and requirements are complete."*

## After the Interview

- Say thank you
- Follow up with your agency after 2-3 days
- Continue applying to other employers — don't put all eggs in one basket

<CalloutBox type="info" title="PRO TIP">
Practice your answers with a friend or family member. Mas confident ka kapag nasanay ka na.
</CalloutBox>

## Conclusion

Ang interview ay opportunity mo to show na ikaw ang best fit para sa job. With proper preparation at confident delivery, makukuha mo ang position na gusto mo.

**Good luck, future OFW!**
